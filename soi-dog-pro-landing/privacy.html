SOI DOG PRO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

SOI DOG PRO ("the app", "we", "us") helps people discover, verify, and fund street-dog rescue missions in Pattaya, Thailand. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we handle

  • Location — With your permission, the app uses your approximate and precise location to show nearby rescue missions on the map. Location is used on-device to center the map and is not sold or shared for advertising.
  • Camera & photos — When you scan a dog, the camera captures an image that is analyzed by an AI model running on your device. The photo is processed locally for verification and is not uploaded unless you choose to attach it to a mission log.
  • Mission & verification activity — When you verify a sighting or interact with a mission, related activity may be stored in our backend (Supabase) to track mission progress and impact.
  • Donations — Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not collect or store your full card details; Stripe handles payment data under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy).

How we use information

  • To show you relevant rescue missions near you.
  • To verify dog sightings and record contributions to missions.
  • To process donations you choose to make.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers that make the app work — Supabase (backend/hosting) and Stripe (payments) — and only as needed to provide those features. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Data retention & deletion

Mission and verification records are retained while they remain useful for tracking impact. To request access to or deletion of your data, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Children

SOI DOG PRO is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
