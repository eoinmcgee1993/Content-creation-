Crypto, markets, business, side hustles, AI income, digital products, freelancing, e-commerce, creator businesses, personal finance, scams and money-making experiments.

Evidence-led reporting on human performance, peptides, PEDs, SARMs, nootropics, research chemicals, longevity, hormones, recovery, alternative medicine, clinical trials and emerging therapies. Reporting separates evidence, anecdote and marketing.

BRIEF, SIGNAL, DEEP DIVE, FIELD NOTE, DOSSIER, THE WEIRD ONE.

## Publishing rule
AI can ingest, cluster, research and draft. Humans approve. Sources remain attached to every story. Unsupported claims block publication.
