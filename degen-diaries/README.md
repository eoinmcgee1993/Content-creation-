# DEGEN DIARIES

**The twice-weekly newspaper for the internet age.**

DEGEN DIARIES is an original digital newspaper built for people who want signal instead of algorithmic sludge. It publishes every Tuesday and Friday across money, technology, human enhancement, culture, strange internet stories and deep investigations.

## Sections

- **MONEY**: Crypto, markets, business, side hustles and money generation ideas
- **MACHINE**: AI, software, robotics and technology
- **BIOHACK**: Human performance, experimental medicine, peptides, PEDs, nootropics, longevity, emerging therapies and the research ecosystem
- **CULTURE**: Music, film, books, fashion and internet culture
- **THE RABBIT HOLE**: Weird, obscure and fascinating stories
- **THE DOSSIER**: One substantial investigation or deep dive

## Editorial engine

Source inbox/newsletters/RSS/URLs -> ingestion -> source extraction -> dedupe -> story scoring -> original rewrite -> fact/source QA -> page composition -> PDF/web -> subscriber email.

AI is newsroom machinery, not the publisher. Every story keeps its source trail. Nothing publishes without editorial QA. Facts, quotes and statistics are never invented.

## MONEY desk

MONEY is not just markets. It covers crypto, business, side hustles, freelancing, digital products, AI income, e-commerce, creator businesses, alternative income, personal finance, scams and money-making experiments.

Recurring formats can include THE SIDE HUSTLE, THE MONEY TEST, €100 -> €1,000, THE OPPORTUNITY, THE GRIFT, AI MONEY and THE RECEIPTS.

## BIOHACK desk

BIOHACK covers human performance and enhancement as an evidence-led research desk, including peptides, PEDs, SARMs, research chemicals, nootropics, longevity, hormones, recovery, alternative medicine, clinical trials and emerging therapies. Coverage distinguishes evidence from anecdote and does not provide unsafe medical instructions or procurement guidance.

## Issue

- Cadence: Tuesday + Friday
- Initial issue size: 12 pages
- Subscription concept: €3.99/month
- Outputs: web edition, A4 PDF/ePaper, mobile reader, email edition and archive

## Principle

**The internet is already noisy. The newspaper should not be.**
