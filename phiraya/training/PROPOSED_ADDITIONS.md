# Phiraya — Proposed Additions (needs owner approval)

These close gaps 11, 12, 14 and 15 in `../REVIEW.md`. Every number and rule here is a **proposal**, not something from the source documents. Edit freely, then fold the approved parts into the training guide.

## 1. Follow-up cadence (gap 11)

| Contact | Touch 1 | Touch 2 | Touch 3 | Then |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thai | Initial message or call | +3 business days, soft (Script A4) | +5 business days, warm check-in with a new option | Pause. Tell the client it is a soft no or bad timing. Re-open only if the contact does. |
| Western | Initial message or call | +2 business days (Script B4) | +4 business days, final "happy to close this out" note | Stop. Report to the client. |

Every follow-up goes in the contact log (section 4). Never follow up on a different channel on the same day.

## 2. Booking brief template (gap 12)

Send to the client 24 hours before and again 1 hour before. Keep it to one screen.

> **Meeting:** [Client] with [Contact, role] · [date, time in both timezones] · [call link or place]
> **Why they agreed to talk:** [one line]
> **Their style:** [e.g. senior, prefers a warm start and small talk / prefers a direct agenda]
> **Address them as:** [Khun First Name / First name]
> **Suggested opening:** [one sentence]
> **Avoid:** [anything sensitive, anything already declined]
> **Confirmed by:** [who said yes, how, when]

## 3. Escalation (gap 14)

Hand to a coordinator, without showing frustration to the contact, when:
- a contact becomes upset, or asks you to stop contacting them (stop at once, log it, tell the client);
- a contact asks for pricing, legal, medical or clinical advice you can't give;
- a booking is missed twice;
- a client asks for something that would pressure or embarrass a contact.

Say to the contact: *"Let me check with the team and come back to you."* This is the guide's own soft phrase and keeps face. Never promise an outcome.

## 4. Contact log (gap 14)

One row per touch: date · agent · client · contact · culture (Thai / Western) · channel · what was said · what worked · what felt uncomfortable · next step and date. Do not record anything beyond what the next agent needs to continue.
