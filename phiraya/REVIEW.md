# Phiraya — Gaps & Contradictions Review

Reviewed: Internal Training Guide v1.1, Cross-Cultural Sales Training v1.0, Call & Email Scripts v1.0, Website & Messaging Copy v1.0, the combined PDF/DOCX digest, and the intro video (frames only). **Blocking issues for publishing the website are in section A.**

## A. Fix before the site or video goes public
1. **Name spelling in the video.** Frame captions read "MEET FIRAYA". The brand is "Phiraya" everywhere else. Confirm captions and voiceover. I could not transcribe the audio, so the voiceover is unchecked.
2. **No contact details.** Every "Get Started" / "Let's Connect" button needs a destination. The only source is the signature template, still `[Phone] | [LINE / WhatsApp] | [Email]`. The site shows "Contact details coming soon" until `CONTACT` in `site/index.html` is filled in.
3. **Unsubstantiated "Available 24/7".** Used in the hero, posts and video. No document says how coverage works across Thai and Western timezones or who staffs it. Don't publish it unless it is true.
4. **"Discreet" for clinics, with no privacy policy.** Clinic work means health-adjacent personal data across borders. No document covers consent, data handling or retention. Thailand's PDPA and the client's own jurisdiction's rules may apply. This needs a policy and a qualified check, and I can't give that advice.
5. **Cold-call compliance is not covered.** Scripts A1/B1 are cold outreach on a client's behalf across two jurisdictions. Do-not-call, consent and recording rules differ by country. Nothing here says who checks them.

## B. Contradictions between documents
6. **Commitment.** Internal Guide: "secure clear commitment while protecting relationships". Sales Training: "do not force a hard yes or no". The merged guide reconciles this (§7), and the owner should confirm.
7. **"Maybe / let me think".** Internal Guide: accept and follow up gently. Sales Training: treat as a soft no or need for more time. Compatible, but agents get two instructions. Merged into one table row.
8. **Version drift.** The Internal Guide is v1.1 (26 Sept); the others are v1.0 (26–27 Sept). The PDF/DOCX digest carries no version.
9. **The digest has drifted from the sources.** It drops the follow-up scripts (A4, B4), the confirmation script on the Thai side (A5), the "Part D" face-saving phrases, "Perhaps we can explore another option", the channel guidance and the Western-side guidance. If agents train from the PDF they get less than the full guide. Use the merged guide as the single source.

## C. Missing content
10. **Language of the Thai-side calls.** All scripts are English, but A1–A5 are calls to Thai contacts. Is the call in Thai or English? No Thai script, transliteration or Thai website copy exists. This is the biggest operational gap.
11. **Follow-up cadence.** "Multiple gentle touches" and "after a few days" are not numbers. Define a cadence per culture, plus when to stop.
12. **The "short brief"** promised in script B5 has no template. The "short summary to both sides" in A5 has none either.
13. **Pricing, packages, service levels, onboarding.** None in any document, so the site has no pricing section.
14. **Escalation and quality.** No rules for a client dispute, a wrong number, an insulted contact or a no-show. The "note what worked" habit has no log format.
15. **Pronunciation** of the name for agents answering phones. Needed given item 1.

## D. Smaller notes
16. The cultural table describes tendencies as if universal. The merged guide adds "mirror the individual", which is a one-line softening and **[Added]**.
17. The first video frame shows two captions overlapping ("EVERY IMPORTANT CLIENT" over "HANDLED."). It may be an intentional cross-fade; I sampled one frame every 5 s and can't tell.
18. The Internal Guide's "Never show frustration" is a good rule, but agents need a way to hand off a difficult call without breaking it (see item 14).
