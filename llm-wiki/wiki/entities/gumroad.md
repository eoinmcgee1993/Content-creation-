# Gumroad

The store where EdgeVault and Growth Fabric are to be sold. Neither is listed
yet.

- Both products are one-time purchases, [[entities/edgevault]] at $49 and
  [[entities/growth-fabric]] at $39, with a $69 bundle planned once both have
  sold [[sources/master-blueprint#Recap and corrections]]
  [[sources/master-blueprint#Launch blockers]].
- Image sizes: Gumroad recommends a cover of at least 1280×720 and a thumbnail
  of at least 600×600 (the blueprint says to check the product editor, since
  specs change). Every image is rendered at twice that
  [[sources/master-blueprint#Gumroad package]].
- The two products share one brand system, so they read as siblings in one
  store [[sources/master-blueprint#Brand system]].
- The store name, support email, refund policy and licence text are the
  owner's decisions [[sources/master-blueprint#Decisions only the owner can make]].

## Listing checklist

From [[sources/master-blueprint#Gumroad package]], for both products:

- [ ] Name, summary and description, pasted from the listing doc
- [ ] Cover images in order, then the thumbnail
- [ ] Product file: the release zip, not a link to the repo
- [ ] Price, with no fake "was" price
- [ ] Licence text, and a refund policy that says plainly what it covers
- [ ] A support email that reaches you
- [ ] One test purchase through Gumroad's own checkout before announcing
      anything
