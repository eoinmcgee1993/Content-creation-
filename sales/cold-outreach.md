# Cold Outreach Kit

Replace every `[BRACKET]`. Lead with a specific, real observation about
*their* account — never a generic pitch. One observation, one offer, one
link. Aim for 20–30 personalized touches/week.

---

## Subject lines (A/B these)

- `[Company] — quick thing I noticed in your [Google/Meta] ads`
- `wasted spend on [Company]'s [campaign type]?`
- `[Company]'s page-2 keywords (quick win)`

---

## Email v1 — paid ads angle

> Hi [First name],
>
> Was looking at [Company]'s ads — you're running [N] creatives on
> [product/category] but they all point to [observation: same landing
> page / no audience exclusions / etc.]. That pattern usually means a
> chunk of spend is converting at a loss.
>
> I run a free 250-point Account Health Check that scores exactly where
> spend is leaking (zero-conversion campaigns, sub-break-even ROAS, CPA
> outliers) with a modeled monthly recovery number.
>
> Sample of the exact report you'd get: [LANDING_PAGE_URL]
>
> Worth a 15-min look at yours?
>
> [Your name]

## Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

- **Day 3:** "Re: [Company] ads — still happy to send the breakdown.
  Want it?" (2 lines, no new ask.)
- **Day 7:** Forward the sanitized sample PDF directly: "Here's what the
  output looks like — took 15 min. Yours would be specific to your
  account."
- **Day 14 (break-up):** "Assuming the timing's off — I'll close this
  out. If account efficiency comes up later, the free check stands."

## Loom video script (45–60 sec)

1. **0–5s:** "Hi [First name], 60 seconds on [Company] — screen-sharing
   what I found."
2. **5–35s:** Show ONE concrete thing on screen: the ads transparency
   page / a page-2 ranking / a landing page mismatch. Name the likely
   cost.
3. **35–50s:** "I packaged a 250-point version of this into a free
   Health Check — scored, with a recovery estimate. Here's the report
   format." (Flash the sample PDF.)
4. **50–60s:** "Link's in the message. Two minutes to grab yours —
   worth a look?"

Keep it unpolished and specific. A real observation beats production
value every time.

---

## Honesty guardrails

- Every observation must be true and verifiable before you send it.
- Use only the sanitized sample report as a "blind example" — never
  rebrand a real client's report.
- The leakage number is a directional model; say so. Don't promise
  guaranteed savings — promise a clear, scored diagnosis.
