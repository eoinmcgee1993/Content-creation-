# AI Account Health Check

## Find where your ad spend and organic traffic are leaking — in days, not quarters.

### The problem

Most accounts quietly bleed budget: campaigns spending with zero
conversions, ROAS below break-even, CPA outliers, keyword
cannibalization splitting authority, and page-2 rankings one push from
page 1. It rarely shows up until someone audits the data directly.

### What it is

A 250-point, rule-based audit across your paid-ads export and Google
Search Console data. You get a scored report (0–100 health score) with a
modeled monthly recovery figure and a prioritized fix list — not a
20-tab spreadsheet.

### What you get

- Executive summary: overall score, critical issues, modeled $/mo leakage
- Paid ads: zero-conversion spend, sub-break-even ROAS, CPA outliers,
  low CTR, budget over-concentration, tracking gaps
- SEO: striking-distance keywords, below-curve CTR, zero-click queries,
  cannibalization clusters, brand-dependency, ranking drops
- A ranked priority list — what to fix first and why

### How it works

1. Free sample report so you see the exact deliverable up front
2. You share a 30-day ad export and/or a GSC export (read-only)
3. You get your scored, personalized report within 48 hours
4. Optional: we implement and manage the fixes on a monthly retainer

### Pricing

- **Health Check:** complimentary (or fixed diagnostic fee)
- **Implementation & management:** monthly retainer scoped to findings

### The honest part

Leakage figures are directional models from your supplied data and
industry benchmarks — a prioritized diagnosis, not guaranteed savings.
Every report ships with its methodology and limits stated plainly.
