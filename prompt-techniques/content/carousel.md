# Carousel (7 slides)

**1 – Cover:** The aerospace trick that fixes your prompts
**2 – Problem:** AI output is long, vague and hard to scan.
**3 – Trick 1:** Ask for ASD-STE100. Short sentences. Plain words. Add "about 80% of the way" so it stays human.
**4 – Trick 2:** Ask for a diagram instead of a wall of text.
**5 – Trick 3:** Ask for HTML/CSS/JS. Get a working tool: sliders, charts, animation.
**6 – Trick 4:** Ask for an explainer script, then add text-to-speech narration.
**7 – CTA:** Try Trick 1 in your next prompt. Credit: Felix Reed, AI News & Chips.

Design notes: one idea per slide, large type, one accent colour. No stats on any slide, because none are verified (see `FACTCHECK.md`).
