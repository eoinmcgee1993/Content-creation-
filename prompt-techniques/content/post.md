# Social post

**Hook:** Aerospace writes manuals so a tired engineer at 3 a.m. can't misread them. You can borrow that for your prompts.

Four prompting tricks from a short video by Felix Reed (AI News & Chips):

1. **Write in ASD-STE100.** It is Simplified Technical English, a controlled language from aerospace. Ask the AI to follow it "about 80% of the way". You get clear text that doesn't read like a robot.
2. **Ask for a diagram, not paragraphs.** Processes and concepts land faster as a picture.
3. **Ask for an interactive page.** Request HTML/CSS/JS and you get a working mini-tool, like an SIP calculator with sliders and a compounding chart.
4. **Ask for an explainer video.** Have the AI script the animation and pair it with a text-to-speech voice for narration.

My take: number 1 is the cheapest to try. Add one line to your next prompt and compare.
