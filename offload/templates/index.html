The problem

Geographic freedom didn't give you psychological freedom. The office walls came down — and so did every boundary between work, worry, and rest.

— Why the overwhelmed can't use ordinary planners

Journaling apps hold your feelings. Task apps hold your to-dos. But an overwhelmed mind can't sort itself into those tidy boxes in the first place — that sorting is the work you can't start. {{ brand }} does the sorting for you.

How it works

Two steps. That's the whole app.

A dump takes fifteen seconds. The clear-out takes one tap. Everything else is deliberately not there.

Step one

Braindump

Hit the mic and talk, or type a line. No prompts, no categories, no tidy sentences. Speaking clears working memory up to three times faster than typing — so we lead with your voice.

Free · forever
Step two

Clear out

One tap sends the mess to the engine. It reads the whole thing and hands back three clean lanes — actions, reframes, and permission to forget the rest.

Powered by the AI engine

The clear-out

Every thought goes to one of three places.

The engine reads one messy dump and routes each thought where it belongs — so nothing important is lost and nothing trivial keeps its grip on you.

What to do

Hidden inside the dread is usually a task. The engine finds it and breaks the scary, vague version into steps small enough to actually begin.

You said“I'm so stressed about the Q3 review, I haven't even opened the deck.” → Open laptop · Find Q2 metrics · Draft a 3-point outline.

What to reframe

Some thoughts aren't tasks — they're distortions. The engine names the trap gently and offers a calmer, truer way to see it.

You said“My manager is going to be furious.” → That's mind-reading. What do you actually know versus what are you fearing?

What to let go

The rest is noise — old cringes, other people's problems, things you can't change. We archive them, and give you the permission slip to stop carrying them.

You said“Still cringing about a joke I made five years ago.” → Nobody remembers but you. This one's safe to set down.

Designed to feel like relief

So it isn't. No streaks to protect. No red badges. No fourteen-tab dashboard. Just a big, calm button and a lot of quiet around it.
