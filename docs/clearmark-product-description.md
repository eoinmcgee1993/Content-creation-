## 3. Short description (100 words)

Clearmark is an Answer Engine Presence (AEP) monitoring platform. It runs your domain through a
structured battery of branded, navigational, and topical queries across the major AI answer
engines, then reports three things: **coverage** (which engines cite you, and for what),
**accuracy** (whether what they say about you is actually true), and **gaps** (where a competitor
is named instead of you). The output is a single AEP score out of 100, backed by a
query-level diagnostic report — not a vanity metric, but a fix list your content team can action.

## 4. Full product description

### The problem

Your buyers have stopped clicking through ten blue links. They ask an AI assistant a question and
act on the answer it gives them. That answer names a small number of brands — and if yours isn't
one of them, you are not "ranking poorly." You are absent from the decision entirely.

This creates a visibility problem that no existing tool measures:

- **Rank trackers don't help.** Position #3 on Google tells you nothing about whether ChatGPT
  recommends you when someone asks for the best tool in your category.
- **The answer is generated, not retrieved.** There is no SERP to inspect. Two users asking the
  same question can get different brands named.
- **Being cited incorrectly is worse than not being cited.** An engine confidently quoting your
  deprecated pricing page actively costs you deals, and nothing in your analytics stack will
  ever surface it.

Marketing teams are being asked "how are we doing in AI search?" and have no instrument to answer.

### What Clearmark does

Clearmark turns AI visibility from a guess into a measurement.

We maintain a structured query battery per engine — branded ("what is *your brand*"),
navigational ("*your brand* pricing"), and topical ("best tools for *your category*") — and run
your domain through it on a recurring schedule. Every response is parsed for whether your domain
was cited, what was said about you, and who was named instead.

The result is your **AEP score**: a 0–100 measure of how present your brand is in generated
answers, decomposed so you can act on it.

### The three pillars

**◈ Citation Coverage — *are you in the answer at all?***
We test a representative set of queries per engine. Your coverage score is the percentage of those
queries where your domain appears in the generated answer, broken out per engine so you can see
that (for example) Perplexity cites you consistently while Gemini never does. Each result records
the query, the engine, whether you were cited, and how prominently.

**◆ Hallucination Detection — *is what they say about you true?***
Answer engines regularly cite brands with incorrect claims: stale pricing, retired product names,
features you never shipped. Clearmark scores citation *accuracy* separately from coverage and
flags every discrepancy with a severity rating and a recommended fix — usually a structured-data
or content correction that gives the engine a better source to draw from. Being cited wrongly is
a distinct failure mode, and we treat it as one.

**◇ Competitive Gap Analysis — *who is being named instead of you?***
For every query where you aren't cited, we record who was. Gaps are aggregated by topic cluster
and engine, so instead of "we're behind in AI search" you get "for *AEP auditing tools* we hold 3
citations to a competitor's 12 — priority HIGH." That maps directly onto a content brief.

### What you get

- **AEP score (0–100)** — one number for the exec update, recalculated every scan.
- **Per-engine breakdown** — coverage and accuracy scored per answer engine.
- **Query-level report** — every query tested, the verdict, and the raw citation context.
- **Hallucination log** — each false claim, its severity, and the recommended remediation.
- **Gap register** — topic clusters where competitors own the answer, ranked by priority.
- **Trend history** — score movement over time, so you can prove the work landed.
- **Verification badge** — a "Verified by Clearmark" mark with your score and last-verified date.
- **Exports** — full report data on every plan. Nothing is locked behind an enterprise gate.

### How it works

1. **Verify your domain.** Enter it — free, no card.
2. **Clearmark builds your query battery.** Branded, navigational, and topical patterns derived
   from your domain and category.
3. **We run the scan** across the supported answer engines.
4. **You get a scored report** — coverage, accuracy, and gaps, with a prioritized fix list.
5. **We re-scan on your schedule** — monthly, weekly, or continuous by plan — and track movement.

### Who it's for

- **Content and SEO leads** who need a defensible metric for AI visibility alongside rankings.
- **Growth and demand-gen teams** watching organic pipeline soften as buyers shift to assistants.
- **Brand and comms** who need to know when an AI is stating something false about the company.
- **Agencies** reporting on a channel their clients are asking about and nobody can yet measure.
- **Founders** in crowded categories where being the named recommendation is the whole game.

### Why not just check manually?

You can ask ChatGPT about your brand once. That's an anecdote. It isn't reproducible, it doesn't
cover the other engines, it won't tell you what changed after you shipped a fix, and it won't
catch the pricing hallucination sitting in a query you never thought to type. Clearmark is the
difference between a spot-check and an instrument.

## 6. Positioning

**Clearmark is to answer engines what rank tracking was to search engines** — the measurement
layer that has to exist before anyone can run a strategy.

Against adjacent categories:

- **vs. rank trackers (Ahrefs, Semrush):** they measure link position on a results page.
  Clearmark measures whether you are named inside a generated answer. Complementary, not
  overlapping.
- **vs. brand monitoring (Brandwatch, Mention):** they track what humans say about you across the
  web. Clearmark tracks what *machines* say about you to buyers at the point of decision.
- **vs. checking by hand:** not reproducible, not multi-engine, not tracked over time.

**Tone:** technical, measured, diagnostic. Clearmark reports findings; it does not hype. The
product's credibility rests on the report being specific enough to act on.

## 8. Open items before this ships as-is

Flagged for the owner — these are claims the current site makes that the product must be able to
stand behind:

1. **Delivery gap.** No answer-engine querying is implemented in the repo. The shipped engine
   (`audits/`) generates **paid-ads leakage + Google Search Console SEO** audits — a different
   product. The lead magnet on the AEP page currently hands prospects an "AI Account Health
   Check" (Google Ads CPA/ROAS/spend report), which will not match what they signed up for.
2. **Testimonials.** The three quotes on the page (Synthex AI, Foundry Labs, Orbital) read as
   placeholders. They are omitted from the deployed page and from this description until real,
   permissioned quotes exist.
3. **"400+ teams."** Unverified. Omitted here; substantiate before using in copy.
4. **Engine naming.** Publicly naming ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Perplexity as monitored targets
   is normal comparative use, but keep claims to observable behavior and avoid implying
   partnership or endorsement.
5. **Score methodology.** The AEP score needs a published, stable definition before it can be
   sold as a metric teams report internally — buyers will ask how it's computed.
