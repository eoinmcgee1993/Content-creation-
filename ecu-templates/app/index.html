2 · Features

Launch control
Secondary rev limiter for hard launches. −5° ignition, +10% fuel at the hold point.
6,500 RPM sits just below peak torque on a stock-displacement CRF — aggressive launch without looping or bogging.
Pops, bangs & flames (decel map)
Throttle closed (0–3% TPS) between 4,500–8,500 RPM: +15% fuel, −2° timing. Unburnt fuel detonates in the hot header on downshifts.
