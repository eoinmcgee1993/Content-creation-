How it works

Calls still come to your clinic's number. Phitara answers the ones your team can't get to.

She answers

Calls to your number that go unanswered, or come in after hours, go to Phitara. She speaks Thai or English, whichever the caller uses.

She books or answers

She answers questions from your clinic's own information (hours, location, services) and books into your open slots.

She follows up

The patient gets a confirmation on LINE. Your team gets a summary of every call, and anything urgent is flagged for a person.

Built for a busy front desk

The receptionists stay. Phitara takes the calls they miss.

🌙

After-hours cover

🗣️

Thai and English

🧑‍⚕️

Hands off to a person

Anything medical, urgent or unclear goes to your staff. She doesn't guess.

The call ends. The conversation moves to LINE.

Patients in Thailand already use LINE. Phitara sends the booking there, so they get a reminder and can reply in the same chat.

  • Confirmation: date, time and location, sent right after the call.
  • Reminder: before the visit, cutting no-shows.
  • Reschedule: the patient replies and Phitara handles it.
